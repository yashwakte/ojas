WHO WE ARE

From a Family Chakki to Your Kitchen

Ojas began in Pune with a simple belief — the food our families eat every day should be as pure as the ingredients that go into it. What started as a small stone-ground chakki serving neighbours has grown into a trusted name for flours and healthy essentials, without ever giving up the traditional methods that make our products what they are.

We source the finest grains from trusted local farms, grind them slowly on traditional stone chakkis to preserve every bit of nutrition and flavour, and pack them fresh — so that what reaches your table is as close to nature as possible.
